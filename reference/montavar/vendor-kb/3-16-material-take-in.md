# 3.16 Material take in
URL: https://websonik.com/kennisbank/3-16-material-take-in/
Last updated 12-06-2026

## Text
- Material receipt of all earlier created material orders happens here. Click the button "Recive materials" (sic, typo in the article; the button reads "Receive materials"), then in the pop-up indicate for which order and which supplier it is, fill in the received quantities, then click "Receive materials" again to save the receipt.
- The article does not mention certificates, negative receipts or numbering at take-in.

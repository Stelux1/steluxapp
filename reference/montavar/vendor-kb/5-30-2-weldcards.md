# 5.302 Weldcards
URL: https://websonik.com/kennisbank/5-30-2-weldcards/ — Updated 06-03-2026
QR codes on Weld Cards (prep for mobile app). Create: Reports menu in an isometric -> 'Weld Card' -> choose 'Include All Welds' or 'Select Specific Weld(s)' -> select -> 'GO'.
Update 05-03-2026: bulk generation of weld labels and weld cards for several isometrics: go to a sub-project, select isometrics, choose labels or cards, start.

## Screenshot findings (from screenshot)
- Reports dropdown on the weld screen: LISL Report, LISL Report [GU], Blank LISL template, Visual inspection, **Weld card**, **Weld labels**, ISO Book (Weld card highlighted).
- Bulk version (screenshot 4): on the Iso overview of a sub-project (Zandbak / Prj.001 / Iso) tick the checkboxes at the right of several isometrics, then dropdown **Reports** offers "Weld card" and "Weld labels". Columns of the iso overview: ISO, IRN, Rev. no., ITP No, Asset tag, Piping class, Drawing no., NoBo, X Ray %, PWHT, Priority, Status (percentage), Started (M/E/W icons), Options.

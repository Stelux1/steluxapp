# 5.110 PQR-list
URL: https://websonik.com/kennisbank/5-110-pqr-lijst/ — Updated 22-07-2024
PQR list = all data about welding procedures qualified by testing (material, parameters, test results).
Fields of the PQR list (16): 1 Material EN/ISO 15608; 2 WPS; 3 Insp. Authority No.; 4 Report No.; 5 Material 1; 6 Filter Material (filler material, label as written); 7 Pipe/Plate; 8 Position (e.g. horizontal, vertical); 9 Welding Process ASME; 10 Shielding Gas; 11 Preheat (°C); 12 Annealing (°C/Time); 13 Impact Test; 14 Hardness Test; 15 Macro; 16 Standard.
Use: open section PQR-lijst in menu; 'Add' new PQR, fill the form; search & filter options on top; 'Save'; edit via pencil icon.
A PQR can be linked to a WPS in the WPS methods screen (specify which PQR belongs to which WPS). The PQR list can be added as an attachment to an ISO Book delivery report (Opleveringsrapport). Benefits listed: QA, traceability, efficiency, compliance. 4 screenshots (list; WPS-PQR linking; ISO book attachment setup).

# 3.11 Spools
URL: https://websonik.com/kennisbank/3-11-spools/
Last updated 02-09-2024

## Text
- The spool function lets you choose between letters and numbers for spool names (Spool A, Spool B, Spool C ... or Spool 1, Spool 2, Spool 3 ...).
- Per project you indicate which spools and names you use, individually or via mass edit. In the "Options" column, tick/untick (check box right of the trash icon) the items to enable/disable for the project site. Then click the gear button "Options" and choose "Activate selected" or "Deactivate selected".
- The article does not mention paint specification or chemical cleaning fields of spools (those appear only as filters on the dashboard, see 3-5-dashboard, and as columns in service-request/laydown/materials-list screens).

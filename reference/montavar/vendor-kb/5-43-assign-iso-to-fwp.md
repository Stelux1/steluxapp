# 5.43 Assign Iso to FWP
URL: https://websonik.com/kennisbank/5-43-assign-iso-to-fwp/ — Updated 12-06-2026
Page lists all isometrics with or without an assigned FWP; tick one or more to move them to an FWP. Steps: select the FWP, click 'Asign Iso to FWP' (sic), click 'Confirm'. One screenshot (Afbeelding147).

# 3.18 Materials list
URL: https://websonik.com/kennisbank/3-18-materials-list/
Last updated 02-09-2024

## Text
- The materials list is a list of all materials chosen at isometric level, with information on whether they are ordered, in stock and picked (taken out).
- For a fast display it is refreshed automatically every night. To refresh by hand, click the refresh button and wait until all lines are refreshed.

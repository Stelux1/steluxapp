# 5.16 Spools
URL: https://websonik.com/kennisbank/5-16-spools/ — Updated 22-08-2024
Spool naming can be letters (Spool A, B, C...) or numbers (Spool 1, 2, 3...). Per project choose which spools/names to use, individually or by mass-edit: in column 'Options' tick/untick items (right of the trash icon), then click the gear 'Options' and choose 'Activate selected' or 'Deactivate selected'.

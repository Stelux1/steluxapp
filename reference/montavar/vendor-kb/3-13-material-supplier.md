# 3.13 Material supplier
URL: https://websonik.com/kennisbank/3-13-material-supplier/

## Text
- Table to link ordered/received materials to a specific supplier, so you can search and follow which materials were delivered by which supplier and whether they meet specification. Suppliers are added manually with "+ Add material supplier" top right.

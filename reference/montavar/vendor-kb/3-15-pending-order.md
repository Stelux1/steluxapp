# 3.15 Pending order
URL: https://websonik.com/kennisbank/3-15-pending-order/
Last updated 02-09-2024

## Text
- Shows what is still outstanding in orders, including (partial) receipt of an order. Use the quick filter to find specific orders or materials.

## Screenshot (from screenshot, very wide image, small text)
- Breadcrumb: project / "Pending order". Panel header "Pending order" with a sort dropdown "Order by date asc." (and a small "x" icon at left of the title). Search box. Columns: Group, Material No, Description, Ordered, Ord. No., Date, Received. Example rows: group BOLTS; HEX NUT THREAD UNC ... ordered 256.00, Ord. No. 00003, date 05.08.2022, received 208; "2 in Stud Bolt ... ASTM A320 Grade L7" ordered 32.00, Ord. No. 00003, 05.08.2022, received 12; several 11/8in, 13/8in, 1 3/4 in stud bolts ordered 8.00 / 20.00 / 24.00 / 28.00, Ord. No. 00007, 02.09.2024, received 0. So a line stays pending until Received reaches Ordered; partial receipts show an intermediate Received count.

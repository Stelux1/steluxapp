Posted 11-09-2022, last updated 12-06-2026

## Text
- The dashboard is the first page after opening MTOPilot. It shows the **spool locations** (not the first phase of the work, but an important part of the process).
- Filters let you quickly find where a given spool is.
- Clicking "Currently on location" opens the spool history with extra info.
- Only a limited number of spools is loaded to keep load time short; "Show all" loads every spool.
- The "Printer" button creates a printable document.
- Tags: locatie, materiaal, spools.
- NOTE: there are no cards/boards/KPI tiles described - the MTO dashboard is a spool-location list.

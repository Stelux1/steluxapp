# 5.31 Reports menu
URL: https://websonik.com/kennisbank/5-31-reports-menu/ — Updated 12-06-2026
Reports menu contains: LISL reports in modern or classic format, with or without NDT/PWHT columns (without = two blank boxes in which heat numbers are entered during prefab) and a LISL blank template. 'Visuele inspectie' (visual inspection) report for one weld, several, or both shop and field. Column **'Vi'** shows if a report exists for welds 01 and 02: dots '...' = no result yet; **'A'** = visual inspection approved.
'ISO Book' report = most comprehensive, many variants; prefab ISO book differs from field ISO book; document selection option to generate a book with chosen documents.
Signature on each page of LISL: Reports -> 'LISL Report' -> choose 'Signature on each page' -> 'Go'.
Light-grey marking of weld fields on a LISL to show which fields must be approved by inspection; available for A4 and A3. Steps: open isometric, Reports, choose which LISL per project need, popup: select 'Prefab', 'Field' or both (nothing = both), 'Go'. Rollout not available to all customers at once.
Auto-add LISL for the workshop (open boxes for heat/charge number) to the prefab/field folder; LISL can also still be generated separately.
Update 05-03-2026: IsoBook can include an NDT procedure as attachment, and export a punch list (blank template or with filled items).
The article does not name the variants [GU],[MV],[ISS],[WQ],[WQ ver 2],[GU Rev 2/3] in text; only 'LISL (GU)' and 'LISL (MV)' appear in 5.02. 13 screenshots (Afbeelding131, 132 and lazy) show the menu — to be read in the appended screenshot notes.

# 3.26 Service companies
URL: https://websonik.com/kennisbank/3-26-service-companies/
Last updated 12-06-2026

## Text
- Helper table of service companies that perform certain operations on materials and spools. The data is needed to pre-fill a service request automatically. Click "Add company", fill the data, click "Add".

## Screenshot (from screenshot)
- Page "Service companies": button "+ Add company", CSV/Excel/print, search. Columns: Name, Address, Phone, Email address, Options (edit, delete). Example rows: Site Groningen, Bilfinger Brabant Mobiel Europoort, Vecom, Brassto, Bilfinger Brabant Mobiel Oosterhout, Staalstraal Brabant, Kuurman Noord BV / SCNN bv, Cuijpers, Henskes, Multinal (some with address/phone/e-mail).
